## 1. Phase 3 implementation verdict

**Core joint EM: conditionally sound in the cases checked; diagnostic contract incomplete.** I traced `fit()` from `estimate` and unit-precision landmark construction through calibration, `costs(lm,b) -> LatticeModel(...,warp="on") -> infer_joint -> JointPosterior.log_a`, then `sufficient_statistics`/`expected_match_energy`, a new E-step at the updated barycenter, and the final E-step at the returned barycenter. For the stated squared-error edge energy, `A` is exactly its correspondence coefficient, so the unchanged precision-weighted M-step is the correct conditional minimizer. The before/conditional/after free-energy check is applied each iteration to this fixed graph objective. `check_joint_invariants` runs at initial, following, and final joint posteriors. The six real fits completed without an invariant/descent exception. Projection uses patient occupancy for retained mass and `A/M` for conditional location; `summarize` uses consensus occupancy for consensus matched support. A small joint fit survived `AlignmentResult.write()` and H5AD readback with joint posterior fields present.

The runtime invariant checks verify row and consensus conservation and matched-state counts, but omit direct `[0,1]` bounds and edge-flow checks. The graph oracle still shares the graph compiler with inference; Phase 3 did not add an independent mixed-case topology/energy oracle. Joint mode still has no `P(no match)` or low-match-count posterior. Its finite-unsupported cell status is a projection-retained-mass rule, not a calibrated patient-level rejection rule. A positive terminal open charge does not preclude all-terminal paths; the real selected fit's source-to-sink all-terminal edge probability was independently reconstructed as effectively zero for all eight patients, but the code's `degenerate_all_gap_risk` flag uses only `patient_unmatched_terminal_mass > 0.9*n` and is not a direct all-terminal probability.

**Concrete diagnostic bug:** in `_project.py:summarize`, joint residual expression is computed as `A[:,j].T @ expression / O_j - B_j`. Under `(2,1)`, `sum_i A_ij` may exceed `O_j`; under `(1,2)` it may be smaller. The expression mean must divide by `sum_i A_ij`, while occupancy `O_j` remains correct for matched-state support. A deterministic `(2,1)` path with two values `x0,x1` gives the current residual `x0+x1-B` instead of `(x0+x1)/2-B`. This does not enter the EM fit or the reported HTAN scoring but makes joint residual diagnostics incorrect.

**Backward compatibility:** relevant tests passed (`295 passed`: joint fit, joint graph, old lattice, old API). A separate numerical comparison of the same old-mode synthetic fit under frozen `e774a9d` and `ecf3134` produced identical SHA256 bytes for barycenter, stacked `log_a`, and projected positions, plus identical free energy. The new default fit has three additional config keys and a changed source fingerprint, so “bit-identical” is accurate for those numerical outputs, not for all serialized metadata. The existing `warp="off"` versus omitted-option test compares two paths through the new source, so this cross-commit check matters. Fit-level synthetic tests establish execution and one favorable stretch case, but the “more paths cannot worsen the achieved EM fixed point” assertion is not a general theorem for separate local EM optima.

## 3. Preregistered decision

**FAIL as a scientific replacement on this cohort.** None of six joint candidates reaches raw purity 0.77150 or paired restricted-unit purity 0.76918; maximum is 0.76793. Coverage is slightly higher (0.8725–0.8775), rejection remains about 0.017%, and held-out concordance rises above raw 0.42106 but **falls below the paired unit baseline 0.43304**. RU1303 overlap falls, but its SCLC-A median moves only from raw 0.74964 to 0.74205 (`o0.02_teq`) or 0.74229 (`o0.1_teq`); the other-patient SCLC-N q10 boundary is near 0.7436. A shift of about 0.007 across this narrow threshold drives much of the binary overlap change. RU1303 cell mean displacement is only 0.009 for `o0.1_teq`, far short of the mid-consensus cost minimum previously diagnosed. This is not a convincing correction of the specific alignment pathology.

Already-correct patients are not uniformly preserved. For `o0.1_teq`, RU581 purity falls from raw 0.865 to 0.843 (paired unit 0.854), and RU831 from raw 0.864 to 0.830 (paired unit 0.819). RU831 coverage rises from 0.49 to 0.58, so this is a real tradeoff rather than a pure rejection artifact. The six configs form a small, committed 3×2 grid and are reasonable as an exploratory screen; the report's post-hoc choice of `o0.1_teq` is identified as a representative, not a passing winner. The missing paired unit row and no-warp joint-graph controls weakened the original evaluation, now supplied in this audit.

## 4. RU831: the “bistability” claim is not supported

The report's RU831 `mean_displacement` and `max_displacement` are **unweighted landmark-conditional** statistics from `quantities.kinetic_curve`, not cell displacement. It averages every finite landmark, even when its posterior matched occupancy is effectively zero. RU831 has 21 landmarks with occupancy `<0.01` in each reconstructed fit. Some of these have occupancy around `1e-15` and a conditional displacement near 0.77; depending on underflow, they are counted as finite or as structural zero. At the identical `o0.1_teq` final cost matrix, NumPy inference yields landmark mean 0.24608 while Numba inference yields 0.11501, with the supported-landmark means both about 0.114. This is a numerical diagnostic artifact, not a demonstrated second EM attractor.

Actual RU831 cell mean displacement is **0.1499–0.1509 in all six fits**, with cell max 0.209–0.210. RU831 projected positions differ by at most 0.0055 across any two candidates; `o0.02_teq` versus `o0.05_teq` differs by at most 0.000155. Matched-row posterior mass changes modestly between those two (`L1(A)=0.328` across 100 rows; max matched-row occupancy difference 0.0038). The MAP path changes gradually: for equal terminal cost, `(1,1)`/internal-patient-gap/`(1,2)` edge counts move from `20/40/40` at omega 0.02 to `24/38/38` at 0.05 and `28/36/36` at 0.1; no second macroscopic path mode is demonstrated. Changing terminal cost at omega 0.1 changes some posterior occupancies more, but cell maps still differ by at most 0.0054. No same-parameter multi-start test or posterior path-family probability was presented. Determinism under identical initialization is not evidence for or against multiple optima.

RU831's selected-fit patient internal unmatched mass is about **37 of 100 landmarks**, versus patient terminal unmatched mass only about **0.25** (0.62 with lower terminal open cost); direct all-terminal path probability is negligible. Thus “cheap terminal edges absorb whole patients” does not explain the observed RU831 behavior. The raw data have 2,261 SCLC-A and **140 SCLC-N cells** for RU831; the latter sit around `s_local≈0.999`. About 94% of RU831 cells are at `s_local<=0.6`, followed by a large pseudotime sampling gap and a small terminal SCLC-N cluster. That supports **incomplete/intermittent local pseudotime support**, not the statement that RU831 biologically lacks an SCLC-N state. This gap and internal unmatched mass are plausible explanations for an apparent warp jump without a branched consensus.

Finally, the report's `endpoint_pileup = O_0+O_last` is endpoint **matched occupancy**, not cell pile-up. In `o0.1_teq`, RU831's fraction of cells at aligned position `>=0.99` is 0.0525 versus 0.0548 raw, so this statistic does not establish a worsened endpoint pile-up.

Other live explanations are local `s_local` sampling gaps, the frozen panel's expression geometry, many internal unmatched states, weak identifiability of match locations at very low occupancy, terminal/open-boundary pricing, and local EM optima. This coarse row-minimum summary does not establish a uniquely multimodal RU831 surface. The revised RU1303 cost surface favors mid-consensus, yet the joint projection barely moves its SCLC-A cells, so objective–posterior–projection coupling remains an open question.

## 6. Minimum discriminating next experiment

**Do not implement a mixture/branch model yet.** A raw RU831 cost heatmap alone is insufficient: it lacks path constraints, posterior mass, cell support and consensus-refit effects. Freeze the 250-gene/unit feature set and one current joint parameter setting; use **RU831, RU1303, and a well-behaved control such as RU581**. For each, compare (i) current joint consensus, (ii) a consensus fit leaving that patient out, and (iii) the same-graph no-warp control with matched terminal pricing. At the same barycenter and penalties, compute rowwise disjoint cost basins **and** top competing complete path-family energies/posterior mass, plus patient/consensus occupancy, internal/terminal unmatched mass, and cell-supported warp maps. Refit the chosen joint setting from two deliberately different barycenter initializations to test true multiple EM optima. Keep these few runs diagnostic, not a new HTAN winner search.

Interpretation: two well-separated, high-posterior path families at the **same** parameters/consensus would support correspondence ambiguity; a stable single path with terminal posterior mass would support missing terminal coverage; a large improvement only under leave-one-patient-out consensus would support barycenter compromise; a smooth supported path under current consensus would support ordinary local warp. RU1303 should be judged by continuous SCLC-A position/separation as well as the thresholded overlap. Use RU581 to reveal whether any remedy damages an already coherent patient. Only after this should a new consensus architecture be considered.

## 7. Ranked issues and exact recommendation

- **Blocker for scientific promotion:** no candidate passes preregistered purity, paired marker, or convincing RU1303-movement criteria. Keep joint warp experimental and do not replace v2.
- **Major:** RU831 “bistability” and branched-consensus inference are unsupported; the reported split is largely a near-zero-occupancy landmark diagnostic artifact. Correct the interpretation before using it to choose architecture.
- **Major:** `warp_frac` mislabels warp-edge count as patient-state fraction; report both with correct denominators. `run_table` and heatmap omit the paired restricted-unit comparator.
- **Major for diagnostics:** joint residual in `summarize()` divides weighted correspondence by occupancy instead of correspondence column sum. This does not invalidate fit metrics but invalidates that residual output.
- **Major verification gap:** graph oracle still shares compiler; joint low-match/no-match posterior semantics remain undefined; all-terminal warning is not a direct path probability. Add independent mixed-path tables and explicit support diagnostics before interpreting rejection as calibrated.
- **Minor:** “endpoint_pileup” is endpoint occupancy, not cell pile-up; rename or separately measure projected-cell endpoint concentration. The old-mode numerical outputs are identical, but configs/provenance are not byte-identical.
- **Informational:** all six fits and marker scores reproduce exactly; the no-warp controls attain comparable or better purity/marker concordance while failing to change RU1303 overlap. The selected fit has no material all-terminal collapse.

**Next action:** publish this corrected audit alongside the existing report, keep the implementation unchanged for now, and run the three-patient fixed-parameter discriminating experiment above. Require evidence for a competing posterior mode or a leave-one-out consensus effect before considering a branch/mixture architecture. Do not enlarge the six-candidate HTAN tuning grid on this cohort.
